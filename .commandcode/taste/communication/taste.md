# Communication Preferences

- Prefers very terse, direct, imperative instructions (e.g., "go", "do it", "continue", "go now code", "check again") and expects the agent to proceed autonomously rather than asking for clarification. Confidence: 0.9
- Wants concise status/result reports after experiments (e.g., "what is the result", "is it good or not"), not long explanations. Confidence: 0.7
