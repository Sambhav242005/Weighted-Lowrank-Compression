Activation-weighted low-rank fitting remains the strongest direction.

This report separates measured results from pending novelty and deployment claims. The primary scope is attention output projections; direct high-sparsity pruning is shown as a negative comparison.

Current decision. On the pinned Gemma-3-1B run, activation-weighted rank-384 fitting averaged about 6.7% PPL increase across three calibration seeds, while matched plain SVD produced 251.52 PPL versus a 58.12 baseline. This supports an empirical advantage over SVD, but not algorithmic novelty until ASVD/EoRA controls are complete.
